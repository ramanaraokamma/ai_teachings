Independent transfer case

Independent case: Compare a new shared sound test

Versions X and Y label the same 20 previously unseen clips: 12 quiet and 8 noisy, with no missing outcomes. X is correct on 10 quiet and 2 noisy clips; Y on 9 quiet and 5 noisy clips. Calculate each combined fraction and percentage, identify the overall leader and compare the leaders within each group. Explain why an overall result cannot guarantee the next clip’s outcome.

Attempt this case before feedback. Record any help. Earlier examples below are further practice; a case already practised with feedback cannot establish fresh transfer.

W1 Explain both parts of a performance fraction

For version A in the lesson, write correct and assessed counts, simplify the accuracy fraction and express it as a percentage. Explain what its denominator includes and why 9/9 would be misleading.

W2 Compare totals and conditions

Compare A and B on the shared 12-card test and on its bright and dim groups. State the leader in each and explain why the overall result does not imply that one version wins in every condition.

W3 Do not hide unassessed outcomes

C has 9 correct and 1 incorrect assessed outcome, with 2 other selected cases unassessed. Write its assessed accuracy and missing count. A reviewer reports 9/12 as assessed accuracy; another reports 90% on all 12. Explain the problem with each label.

W5 Combine counts rather than group percentages

A separate test has 2 correct out of 2 assessed in group P and 4 correct out of 8 assessed in group Q. Calculate combined accuracy as a fraction and percentage. A chart reports 75% by averaging the group percentages. Explain why that is not the accuracy over all assessed cases.

Pictorial case practice: Fair for Whom?

A fictional badge reader is evaluated on bright and dim cards with known reference labels.

Version M is correct on 9 of 10 bright cards and 3 of 6 dim cards.

Version N uses the same cards: 8 of 10 bright and 5 of 6 dim correct.

Which group loses performance under N, and can total improvement erase that fact?

Support used: alone / hint / worked example. This record is practice evidence.

Project connection: Report sorter outcomes by relevant conditions and record whose failures matter when choosing a revision.
