02. Follow the deciding steps

Count objects: Two bottles and one beaker satisfy the count requirement. → Check position: Bottles on the right fail the required left-of relation. → Check direction: Arrow from beaker to bottles reverses the required flow. → Repair brief: Specify bottles-left, beaker-right, arrow left-to-right; verify the revised output.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.
