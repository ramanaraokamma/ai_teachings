04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: A sentence promising snacks has no supporting record. Changed condition: Reserve four places instead of two, keeping the room and day unchanged. Expected result: Eight learner places remain.

Reason: 3×4−4=8; this changes capacity for learners, not the retrieved date or location.
