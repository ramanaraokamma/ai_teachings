After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Numerical check With step 0.1: w losses 0.64, 1.44 give -4; b losses 0.81, 1.21 give -2. A gradient lists the slope for each parameter, in a declared order. Changing w by a small amount changes the prediction twice as much as changing b by the same amount. That explains the different derivative magnitudes. Perturb one coordinate while holding the other fixed, then compare the numerical slope with its analytic counterpart.
