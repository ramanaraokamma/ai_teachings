02. Follow the deciding steps

Multiply inputs: 2×0.5=1 and 1×(-1)=-1. → Add weighted terms: Pre-activation z is 1-1+0.2=0.2. → Apply ReLU: max(0,0.2)=0.2; this activation is not a confidence percentage.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: max(0,0.2)=0.2; this activation is not a confidence percentage. Changed condition: Change the second input from 1 to 2. Expected result: z becomes 1-2+0.2=-0.8 and ReLU returns 0.

Reason: The negative weighted term grows; ReLU clips a negative pre-activation to zero.

Predict before checking the worked changed-case result. Name what changed and what the supplied facts allow you to conclude.

Limit: This one-neuron arithmetic does not establish how a trained image classifier generalises or why its full network makes a decision.
