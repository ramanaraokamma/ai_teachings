W1 · Explain the current example

Keep this task’s inputs,rule and response together.

Complete task and evidence

  • An ablation removes a component while preserving evaluation cases, scoring and other settings. Main full system has retrieval and validation with 8/10 passes. Without retrieval it has 5/10, a net drop of three passes or thirty percentage points. Without validation it has 7/10, a drop of one pass or ten points, and a stipulated unauthorized action. The action boundary is not captured by score magnitude alone.

    Joint removal is not supplied. If its count were hypothetically 4, the interaction contrast 8 − 7 − 5 + 4 would be zero; if hypothetically 6, it would be two. Neither value is observed. Thus the supplied three cells do not determine interaction or additive component contributions.

    Separate filter example has 8/10 full versus 6/10 without the filter: a net two-pass, twenty-percentage-point drop, or 25% relative decrease. Net counts do not identify exact flipped cases. Also changing the model confounds filter-only attribution. These supplied summaries establish no actual model execution, significance or universal causal effect.

    Preserve conditions and inspect per-case failure categories when records exist.
