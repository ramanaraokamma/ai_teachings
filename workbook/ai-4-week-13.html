Rules supplied for this independent case

Pipeline rules: fit each feature’s minimum and range (maximum minus minimum) using training rows only. Scale every training and query component as (value − fitted minimum) / fitted range, without clipping. For k=1, sum absolute component differences and break distance ties by alphabetical ID. The baseline is the training-majority label. Do not refit on validation queries.

Use P (0,60) blocked, Q (20,20) clear, R (20,60) clear as new training rows. Fit the same pipeline. Predict validation queries (2,28),(18,56),(4,12) with references blocked,clear,blocked.
