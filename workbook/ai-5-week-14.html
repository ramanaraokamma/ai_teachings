After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Inspect paths Both end at 3 and loss 1; larger rate crosses the optimum while smaller rate stays below it. Keep the starting parameter and update budget identical to compare rates fairly. Rate one-quarter approaches the optimum from below; rate three-quarters crosses it and comes back. Their final losses happen to match after two updates, despite different paths. Rate one repeatedly overshoots by the same distance, so using the correct gradient alone does not ensure improvement.
