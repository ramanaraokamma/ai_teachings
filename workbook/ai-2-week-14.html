Candidates ask colour_cover or diagram_inside; each leaf uses its training majority. A leaf tie chooses No. Select fewer training errors.

Point to the given fact that matters most. Explain why it matters.

02. Follow the deciding steps

Colour candidate: Both colour leaves tie Yes/No, choose No: two training errors. → Diagram candidate: Diagram 1 leaf predicts Yes; diagram 0 leaf predicts No: zero errors. → Select tree: Choose the diagram question using the training comparison. → Apply new input: A new (1,0) row follows diagram=0 and predicts No.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.

03. Two paths through the learned tree

Two paths through the learned tree. Is near equal to 1 | No near is 0 | Predict clear. Is near equal to 1 | Yes near is 1 | Predict blocked

Read the labels and values. Explain the relationship the model shows.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: A new (1,0) row follows diagram=0 and predicts No. Changed condition: New input becomes (0,1). Expected result: The fitted tree predicts Yes.

Reason: It follows diagram=1; cover colour is not the selected question.

Predict before checking the worked changed-case result. Name what changed and what the supplied facts allow you to conclude.
