Workbook · 13 of 18

W2 Keep the final evaluation record

Use a separate sheet for the teacher’s reserved cases. For every case record its ID and inputs, catalogue and candidate labels, both methods’ observed outputs and reasons, and results after checking. Preserve failure traces. Here summarise each method’s full passes over attempted cases and list unrun cases. State what the comparison supports.

W3 Prepare your evidence defence

Write a two-minute explanation of the problem, one trace, your actual comparison, a limitation and a next action. Explain why this is a written-rule design rather than a model trained on examples. State which personal information is unnecessary and who decides whether to act.

Two additional planned cases are unrun. No timing or pupil-satisfaction measures were collected.

Write one defensible success statement and one claim the evidence cannot support.
