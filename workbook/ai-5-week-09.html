Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Check small move Move to a = 0.25: loss 3.0625, below starting loss 4. A slope describes how the loss changes near the current parameter. The symmetric calculation compares equal small moves on either side. Its negative result suggests a small increase may help at zero. It does not promise every increase helps: jumping to five passes the optimum at two and produces a larger loss. Check the new loss after each proposed step.
