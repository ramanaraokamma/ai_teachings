03. Paired overall and missing-source passes

Paired overall and missing-source passes. P: Overall passes /7 = 4, Missing-source passes /2 = 1. Q: Overall passes /7 = 5, Missing-source passes /2 = 0.

Read the labels and values. Explain the relationship the model shows.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: Q improves overall count but worsens missing-source behavior; use the declared priorities. Changed condition: Make missing-source correctness a required selection gate.

Expected result: Neither prompt clears a 2/2 gate on these records. Reason: P fails 7 and Q fails both 4 and 7; both need revision.
