01. Source Quality: the case

A fictional Study Buddy answers which room a club is authorised to use. Supplied facts: Signed notice A: Hall 1 from day 1. Signed amendment B references A and changes to Hall 3 effective day 8.

An old copy of A is reuploaded on day 10. B remains the authoritative amendment. Queries concern day 6 and day 9.

Point to the given fact that matters most. Explain why it matters.

02. Follow the deciding steps

Check authority: B is a signed amendment to A, not an unrelated comment. → Day 6: Before effective day 8, authorised room is Hall 1. → Day 9: From day 8 onward, authorised room is Hall 3. → Ignore upload shortcut: Reuploading old A on day 10 does not revoke B.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.

03. Conflicting and time-sensitive sources — evidence trace

Conflicting and time-sensitive sources — evidence trace. Notice N says room A from Monday. Signed amendment M references N and changes to B effective Wednesday.

Query asks Tuesday, then Thursday. | For this toy task a signed amendment supersedes the referenced original from its effective time. Keep both records.

A newer timestamp alone has no authority unless the policy grants it. | Tuesday uses A; Thursday uses B. The amendment effective time, not merely upload order, determines the change.

Read the labels and values. Explain the relationship the model shows.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: Reuploading old A on day 10 does not revoke B. Changed condition: A signed amendment C explicitly revokes B effective day 12 and restores Hall 1. Expected result: Day 9 stays Hall 3; day 13 becomes Hall 1.

Reason: The new authoritative change applies from its stated effective date, not retroactively.

Predict before checking the worked changed-case result. Name what changed and what the supplied facts allow you to conclude.

05. Connect the explanation to its limit

A source’s upload time and the time its rule takes effect answer different questions. Preserve version relationships and the query’s intended date. The current authoritative chain gives a consistent answer to both queries.

A later filename or timestamp alone cannot establish that an old record supersedes a signed amendment. Limit: Authority is declared in this fictional case; real conflicting sources may require the responsible owner’s review.

Explain the case, then point to a limit on the claim.
