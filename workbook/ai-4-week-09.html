W4 Inspect and document independently

A new fictional indoor trip export has (P,4), (Q,None), (Q,None), (R,-1), (S,6), (T,0), with durations in minutes and the same schema. Count raw rows, distinct IDs, missing durations, negative durations and extra ID occurrences. Identify overlap. Draft a short dataset card stating source, row meaning, unit, findings, unchanged version and one excluded use.
