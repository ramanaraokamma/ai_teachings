Four-step worked evidence for Week 14: Scores and Calibration Intuition. 1 | Each score estimates the probability that the selected label is correct.

Ten predictions have such scores near 0.8.. 2 | Only six are correct, so observed correctness is 6 ÷ 10 = 60%.. 3 | The score ranking may still help, but 0.8 should not be stated as an 80% guarantee.. 4 | Use bins across many cases and design an uncertain zone requiring human review.
