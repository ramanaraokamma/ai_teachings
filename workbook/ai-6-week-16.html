W1 · Explain the current example

Keep this task’s inputs,rule and response together.

Complete task and evidence

  • Main virtual policy: at most 3 total attempts, each capped at 4 seconds of caller waiting, with gaps 1 and 2 seconds before attempts 2 and 3. Under enforced local caps and excluding overhead, bound = 3×4+1+2 = 15 seconds. Three timeouts exhaust the budget; no fourth attempt is started. Only declared timeout/transient categories retry in this local model; authentication, permission and invalid-request failures stop.

    Separate read-only picture: two attempts with 1-second caps and zero gap; first timeout 1 second plus supplied success .4 second totals 1.4 seconds. Cancellation before attempt 2/backoff stops future work at 1 second. Helper validates finite nonnegative numeric durations excluding bool, exact integer attempt count and one fewer gap than attempts.

    Code adds virtual durations only, with cancellation at supplied between-attempt boundaries. Caller timeout/cancellation does not prove remote work stopped or undo committed effects. No real sleep, network, clock measurement or asynchronous cancellation is implemented.
