Changed ten-test family has cutoff 0.005, with four outcomes missing; only 0.004 among supplied values rejects, and the other four decisions are unknown. Keep those tests in the denominator and disclose missing results. The scenarios are stipulated, not actual experiments.

    An adjusted p-value is min(1, m × p). Adjustment cannot validate invalid p-values, hide outcome-driven selection, prove non-rejected nulls or establish useful effect sizes. The error bound can be conservative and is not an observed error rate.

01. Multiple comparisons: the case

Six declared prompt comparisons. A team performs six valid hypothesis tests and applies a predeclared Bonferroni familywise threshold. Supplied facts: Familywise alpha=0.05.

Supplied unadjusted p-values are 0.004,0.012,0.02,0.08,0.4,0.9. These values are stipulated, not calculated from invented data.

Point to the given fact that matters most. Explain why it matters.

02. Follow the deciding steps

Count the planned tests: There are six tests in the declared family. → Compute corrected threshold: 0.05/6 is about 0.00833 for each test. → Apply all six decisions: Only 0.004 is below 0.00833; the other five do not reject under this rule.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.

03. Supplied p-values and six-test decisions

Supplied p-values and six-test decisions. Test 1: Supplied p = 0.004, At cutoff 0.00833 = Reject. Test 2: Supplied p = 0.012, At cutoff 0.00833 = Do not reject. Test 3: Supplied p = 0.02, At cutoff 0.00833 = Do not reject.

Test 4: Supplied p = 0.08, At cutoff 0.00833 = Do not reject. Test 5: Supplied p = 0.4, At cutoff 0.00833 = Do not reject. Test 6: Supplied p = 0.9, At cutoff 0.00833 = Do not reject.

Read the labels and values. Explain the relationship the model shows.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: Only 0.004 is below 0.00833; the other five do not reject under this rule. Changed condition: The declared family has ten tests instead of six; keep the listed values unchanged.

Expected result: The cutoff becomes 0.005, and only 0.004 among the listed values still rejects. Reason: The error allowance is divided among ten tests; all ten results must be reported, including the four not supplied here.

Predict before checking the worked changed-case result. Name what changed and what the supplied facts allow you to conclude.

05. Connect the explanation to its limit

Searching many comparisons provides more chances for false positives. Bonferroni divides the error allowance across the declared family; valid individual p-values are required.

A rejected null is not automatically a large or useful improvement. Limit: No effect size or real data is supplied.
