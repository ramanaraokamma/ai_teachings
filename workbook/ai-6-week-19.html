Bound a student program. A toy sandbox forbids network access, allows at most two seconds and limits output to 20 MB.

Practice 1: explain the deciding evidence

Which labelled step matches this detail? Stop the prohibited network action regardless of its purpose.

1. Deny B network call

2. Apply isolation

3. Check A

Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Deny B network call Stop the prohibited network action regardless of its purpose. Sandbox controls bound reach and resource use. Passing an output limit cannot cancel a network or time restriction.

Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: A takes three seconds but still writes 2 MB. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?

1. This one example guarantees correct results for every future input.

2. Stop A for exceeding the time allowance.

3. We can decide the result without examining the changed input or the procedure.

Hint if needed: Use the changed condition and the deciding step in the pictorial trace. The answer must say what follows from those facts.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Stop A for exceeding the time allowance. The independent two-second limit remains binding.
