W1 · Explain the current example

Keep this task’s inputs,rule and response together.

Complete task and evidence

  • Main fictional job may read fixture area, write output area, use no network, run within a declared 2-second cap and have at most 10 queued tasks. Local handles fixtures/outputs/outside represent permission classes, not real paths. Read fixtures and write outputs are declared pairs; network public_web, write outside and write fixtures are denied. Public availability does not grant network authority.

    Main all-or-nothing queue fixtures: 8+2=10 accepts, 8+3=11 rejects before changing the queue; no atomic coordinator is implemented. Separate picture permits at most 2 seconds and 20 MB, where 1 MB means one million bytes, and forbids network access. Trial A at 1 second/2 MB fits supplied limits; trial B network request must be denied; changed A at 3 seconds/2 MB exceeds time.

    Equality at 2 seconds/20 MB fits; 20 MB plus 1 byte exceeds output. These are policy calculations and declared trial records, not actual process/network/filesystem enforcement or a correctness/security proof.
