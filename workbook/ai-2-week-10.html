Rows J=(mystery,True,100), K=(mystery,False,90), L=(science,True,80), M=(mystery,True,blank). Missing required fields request review.

02. Follow the deciding steps

Read all conditions: Three requirements must pass, not just a high preference score. → Trace J: Mystery, illustrated and exactly 100 pages: choose. → Trace K and L: K fails illustration; L fails genre: do not choose. → Trace M: Missing pages means review rather than assuming it is short.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.

03. Three records through the Tower1 classifier

Three records through the Tower1 classifier. 5 cm | Accepted; 5 is below 6 | Tower1 predicts short. 6 cm | Accepted; 6 is at least 6 | Tower1 predicts tall.

Height blank | Missing height; stop before rule | Tower1; no prediction
