Figures make evidence easier to inspect when labels and scales preserve meaning. A dramatic-looking visual cannot supply missing uncertainty or representativeness.

Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: Start the axis at 5 without making the truncation obvious. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?

2. The bars exaggerate the visual contrast and require correction or clear disclosure.

3. We can decide the result without examining the changed input or the procedure.
