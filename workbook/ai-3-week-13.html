W3 Audit the limit

A report omits this qualification: A stage-level accuracy or authority policy does not establish every modality agrees or every downstream claim is supported. Explain how omitting it could change a reader's interpretation, and write an accurate replacement.

W5 Design a revealing follow-up

Pictorial case practice: Multimodal Evidence

A fictional Study Buddy audits a desk description and a separate caption.

Description: one open transparent box visibly contains three pencils; one closed opaque box hides its contents.

Caption: “There are five pencils on this desk.” No other evidence reveals the closed box.

Why were both “exactly three” and “exactly five” unsupported before opening the box?

Support used: alone / hint / worked example. This record is practice evidence.

Project connection: Label Study Buddy claims by their visual, audio or text evidence and retain uncertainty when modalities omit information.
