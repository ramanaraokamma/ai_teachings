Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Update and verify v becomes1; prediction 3 and loss 0. Next gradient is 0. Only the output weight v is being fitted in this toy network; the hidden ReLU is fixed. The training input gives hidden value three. Squared-error slope points v from zero towards one, and this specially chosen rate reaches one in a single update. Zero loss on one training pair is exact fitting of that pair, not evidence about other inputs.

Changed-case practice: Change target from 3 to6 and restart v = 0 with the same rate. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?
