After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Handle duplicate Return the stored K9 result without another reservation. Approval answers whether an action may proceed. Idempotency prevents repeated handling of that action from creating duplicate effects.

Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: A new K10 asks for desk 5 without approval. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?

1. Stop K10 at the approval gate.

2. We can decide the result without examining the changed input or the procedure.

3. This one example guarantees correct results for every future input.

Hint if needed: Use the changed condition and the deciding step in the pictorial trace. The answer must say what follows from those facts.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Stop K10 at the approval gate. K9’s approval does not apply to a different action.
