Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Compare collapsed summary If the head receives only h1+h2, both become3 and the target distinction is lost. A hidden representation decides which distinctions remain available to an output head. These two hidden coordinates preserve whether the left or right side is brighter. Adding them discards that direction and gives both examples the same scalar. A deterministic head cannot assign different labels to identical inputs; retain the distinction or change the task.

Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: The head keeps both ordered hidden values instead of their sum. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?
