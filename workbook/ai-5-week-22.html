W1 · Explain the current example

Keep this task’s inputs,rule and response together.

Complete task and evidence

  • Main map [[1, 4, 2, 0], [3, 2, 5, 1], [0, 6, 1, 3], [2, 4, 2, 7]]. Use 2×2 windows, stride 2, no padding, complete windows only. Starts (0, 0), (0, 2), (2, 0), (2, 2) have sums 10, 8, 12, 13; maxima 4, 5, 6, 7; means 2.5, 2, 3, 3.25. The 2×2 outputs preserve coarse region positions and lose detail within a window. Different positions of one 4 among zeros give max 4 and mean 1.

    Two bottom-position windows with one 6 give max 6 and mean 1.5. Window [[-4, -2], [-7, -3]] has max -2 and mean -4. The 3×3 example with a 99 border reads only [[1, 2], [3, 4]], giving max 4 and mean 2.5. Moving an 8 from column 1 to 2 in a 2×4 zero map changes max [[8, 0]] to [[0, 8]] and mean [[2, 0]] to [[0, 2]]. The explicitly declared left-positive head changes class 1 to 0.

    The separate picture uses only the top two main rows and has 1×2 outputs; swapping 1 and 4 inside its left window leaves both summaries unchanged.

  • For the lesson window starting at [2, 2], list the four values, sum, maximum and mean. State the complete pooled output shape.

Practice 1: explain the deciding evidence

Check after the first attempt

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Compare summaries Max map [[4, 5]], mean map [[2.5, 2]], both shape 1×2. Pooling compresses each local window into a summary.

Maximum keeps its largest value; mean keeps its average. Neither summary records where each value appeared.

Swapping values inside one window leaves both summaries unchanged here, so the pooled output cannot reconstruct the original arrangement. The chosen window, stride and boundary policy determine the output shape.

05. Connect the explanation to its limit

Pooling compresses each local window into a summary. Maximum keeps its largest value; mean keeps its average. Neither summary records where each value appeared.

Swapping values inside one window leaves both summaries unchanged here, so the pooled output cannot reconstruct the original arrangement. The chosen window, stride and boundary policy determine the output shape.

Limit: Pooling is a declared aggregation, not automatic improvement; losing location can harm tasks that need precise positions.
