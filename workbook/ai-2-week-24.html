01. Feedback Loops: the case

A fictional catalogue repeatedly recommends two books using observed clicks. Supplied facts: Initially each click count is 0. Equal counts choose earlier ID A over B. Only the recommended book is shown.

In an invented three-round record, A is shown and clicked each round; B is never shown.

Point to the given fact that matters most. Explain why it matters.

02. Follow the deciding steps

Initial tie: Counts A=0,B=0; ID rule shows A. → Record event: A receives a click, so its count becomes 1; B stays 0. → Repeat selection: Higher A count causes A to be shown again; after three clicks A=3,B=0. → Interpret carefully: B’s zero may mean no exposure, not dislike.

Point to each numbered stage and explain which recorded input it uses.

03. Exposure and clicks after the invented three rounds

Exposure and clicks after the invented three rounds. A: Shown = 3, Clicked = 3. B: Shown = 0, Clicked = 0.

Read the labels and values. Explain the relationship the model shows.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: B’s zero may mean no exposure, not dislike. Changed condition: On a planned trial, show B once and record no click. Expected result: B gains one exposure but still zero clicks.

Reason: The observation now supports a nonclick on that display, not a permanent dislike.

Predict before checking the worked changed-case result. Name what changed and what the supplied facts allow you to conclude.

05. Connect the explanation to its limit

The selection policy changes which evidence is available. A accumulates clicks partly because the system gives it every opportunity, while B has none. These records cannot compare enjoyment of two equally exposed books.

Keep display counts separate from click counts; investigate alternatives with a teacher-approved exposure plan rather than treating zero as negative feedback.

Limit: A click can reflect curiosity or a mistake; even equal exposure does not reveal every reason for selection.

Explain the case, then point to a limit on the claim.
