After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Compute grouped result Parentheses give 3+2=5 first; multiplying by 5 gives 25. Parentheses explicitly group an operation. Without them, Python multiplication takes priority over addition; the same numerals can therefore describe different procedures.
