Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: B sounds friendlier but has the same unsupported count as A. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?

1. We can decide the result without examining the changed input or the procedure.

2. The stated improvement hypothesis is not supported on these cases.

3. This one example guarantees correct results for every future input.

Hint if needed: Use the changed condition and the deciding step in the pictorial trace. The answer must say what follows from those facts.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: The stated improvement hypothesis is not supported on these cases. Friendliness is a different outcome from the predefined unsupported-case count.
