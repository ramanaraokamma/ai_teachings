Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Check length boundary Length is 3, but index 3 is beyond the final valid positive index 2. List positions begin at zero. A negative index counts from the end; the list length counts entries and is not itself a valid positive index for the final entry.
