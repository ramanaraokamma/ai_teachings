W1 · Explain the current example

Complete task and evidence

  • Robustness checks compare a frozen procedure under declared varied conditions. Keep baseline, scoring and eligibility aligned and report slices separately. Main current-source pass rate is 9/10 = 90%, outdated-source 5/10 = 50%, a forty-percentage-point descriptive gap. This association does not identify a cause. Separate frozen-classifier picture has indoor 7/8 = 87.5% and outdoor 3/8 = 37.5%, gap fifty points.

    Pooling yields 10/16 = 62.5%; reporting only that value hides outdoor weakness. The observed case mix need not match future use. Eight cases per slice do not establish stable population rates, significance or universal robustness. Aggregate counts cannot reconstruct individual paired transitions. New conditions extend the evidence only to their tested scope.
