Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Check second reply It is short, but room D contradicts room B. A behaviour contract can accept different wording while requiring the same facts. Short, fluent text may still fail factual support.

Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: Use a 25-word answer that preserves both facts. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?

1. We can decide the result without examining the changed input or the procedure.

2. This one example guarantees correct results for every future input.

3. It passes support but fails the length requirement.

Hint if needed: Use the changed condition and the deciding step in the pictorial trace. The answer must say what follows from those facts.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: It passes support but fails the length requirement. Every mandatory criterion must pass; facts do not erase a length violation.
