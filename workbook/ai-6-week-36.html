Defend one complete service trace. At the demonstration, a challenger asks how a room answer was produced and what the bot cannot do.

Practice 1: explain the deciding evidence

Which labelled step matches this detail? With no approved card, decline to invent a room.

1. Name missing evidence

2. Show source path

3. Support the claim

4. Show authority

Hint if needed: Read the steps in the pictorial case. Match the supplied detail to the stage that performs or records it.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Name missing evidence With no approved card, decline to invent a room. A defence links behaviour to evidence and limitations. Demonstrate both a valid answer path and a meaningful refused request.

Practice 2: explain the deciding evidence

Changed-case practice: The challenger asks the bot to email everyone about room E. Which conclusion is supported by the supplied facts?

1. This one example guarantees correct results for every future input.

2. Refuse that operation while retaining the supported room answer.

3. We can decide the result without examining the changed input or the procedure.

Hint if needed: Use the changed condition and the deciding step in the pictorial trace. The answer must say what follows from those facts.

After your attempt, compare with the supported explanation: Refuse that operation while retaining the supported room answer. Knowing a fact does not grant authority for an external action.
