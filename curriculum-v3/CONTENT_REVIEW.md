# Curriculum 3 content and presentation review

The review covers all 252 planned weeks across Grades 6–12. Every week was screened against its planned title, learning structure, defined vocabulary, lesson/workbook/teacher alignment, guided responses, independent assessment, teaching sequence, misconceptions and reviewed diagram artifacts. Editorial changes target the weak explanations and presentation issues found during that screening.

This is an editorial and technical review, not a classroom study establishing comprehension for every learner. The source screens run on every week; detailed editorial expansions and visual inspections are recorded separately below.

## Improvements

- All 252 student study editions place vocabulary before technical reading, provide a section map, show diagram text, and include workbook response space.
- All 252 teacher study editions include a topic primer, vocabulary, lesson-preparation material, the two-session plan, explanations, misconceptions, support and independent-assessment guidance.
- Grade 7 Weeks 10–36 have 27 added plain-language worked explanations.
- 47 brief teacher answers have been expanded into step-by-step reasoning across Grades 7–10.
- 96 weeks (Grade 10 Weeks 13–36 and all Grade 11–12 weeks) have specific, observable learning targets instead of the repeated generic targets.
- The same 96 evidence traces now provide complete input, procedure and conclusion text alongside the abbreviated diagrams.

## Collections

- `outputs/curriculum-reviewed/student-study-complete.zip`: 252 offline student editions, with lessons and workbook prompts.
- `outputs/curriculum-reviewed/teacher-study-complete.zip`: 252 offline teacher preparation editions, including solutions.
- Extract either archive and open `index.html`. Each chapter is self-contained; use its print button to print or save as PDF.
- These enriched HTML study editions supplement the original reviewed Word/PDF collections in `outputs/curriculum-complete`. The Word/PDF collections retain their original reviewed bytes; the extra explanations are in the study editions and local portal.
- `outputs/curriculum-reviewed/week-by-week-review.json` records the per-week evidence, source hashes and archive-file hashes.

## Validation

- All 252 per-week source and artifact screens passed.
- Both audience ZIPs are verified against their file-hash manifests.
- 1,008 desktop/mobile layout checks cover all 504 audience editions at 1280px and 390px: no horizontal page overflow, broken diagrams, missing section anchors or page JavaScript errors.
- Seven representative weeks span all grades, with student and teacher screenshots and browser-print samples. Visual inspection is sampled rather than a claim that every printed page was inspected.
- Existing content checks cover capstone arithmetic, attention, retrieval, service boundaries, sampling, intervals and exact tests.
- The final production build, TypeScript check and all 33 access-test groups passed, including teacher-solution separation and original download hashes.
- After the final Grade 10 target changes, 96 affected desktop/mobile audience layouts were rechecked and passed.
- All 136 pages of 14 sampled browser-print PDFs passed canonical-text and text/image-boundary checks. All nine print contact sheets were visually inspected; diagrams, tables, code and response spaces remained within their pages.
