Main lesson · Section 2

Organise the defence around evidence-backed claims

A polished demo is one observation, not a complete evaluation.

Understand a defensible presentation

A creator defence explains the problem, design choices, source boundaries, implementation and evidence to someone who has not seen the project. Demonstrate a supported path and a missing-answer path. Separate retrieval, supported answers, fallback and allowed actions; they are different outcomes. State which results were executed, traced on paper or supplied for practice.

A clear account of a failure and its limits is stronger evidence of understanding than an invented perfect result.

A generative-system defense presents the question, architecture, corpus, permissions, baseline, evaluation and unresolved failures. Demonstration fluency is only one observable property.

Label fictional practice separately from project results

Begin by writing the question the procedure is intended to answer. Identify the information already supplied, the information it will produce and the information still missing.

These are constructed classroom examples; they do not require live accounts, private records or an external service. Do not treat a plausible explanation as a measured outcome.
