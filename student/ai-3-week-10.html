    Core vocabulary

    WordMy own exampleNot this / boundary
    latent representationAn internal representation learned or constructed by a model rather than directly supplied as the original observation.
    noiseVariation or interference that obscures the information relevant to a task.
    denoisingReducing noise in a representation; some generative models learn repeated denoising steps to create outputs.
    conditioningMaking a model's output depend on supplied information, such as a prompt or image.
