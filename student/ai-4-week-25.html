How the mechanism works

UNDER THE HOOD Screening task treats missed positives as costly, so emphasize recall and human follow-up. Recommendation task may track precision among shown items plus user benefit.

Draw a fresh data → model → prediction → evaluation trace:

A closer explanation

Six records have score/reference pairs A .9/blocked, B .7/clear, C .6/blocked, D .4/blocked, E .3/clear, F .1/clear. At threshold .5, TP=2, FP=1, FN=1, TN=2. At .3, TP=3, FP=2, FN=0, TN=1.

Equality is positive, so E is flagged at .3.

scores = [0.9, 0.7, 0.6, 0.4, 0.3, 0.1]
actual = [True, False, True, True, False, False]
for threshold in [0.5, 0.3]:
    predicted = [s >= threshold for s in scores]
    fp = sum(p and not a for p, a in zip(predicted, actual))
    fn = sum(not p and a for p, a in zip(predicted, actual))
    print(threshold, fp, fn, fp + 4 * fn)
# Output rows: 0.5 1 1 5, then 0.3 2 0 2.
