Understand a buildable brief

A capstone design turns a user need into a bounded, testable service. Record the user task, permitted source versions, simpler baseline, input/output contract, missing-evidence behavior, privacy/access limits and review owner. Link each requirement to a test before choosing extra features. Visible worked examples are development practice, not a hidden final evaluation.

A clear brief makes a build reviewable; it does not prove that the implementation will meet it.

A grounded-assistant capstone begins with bounded requirements and a versioned permitted corpus. A baseline can be a manual lookup process using the same evidence.

Use supplied fictional design inputs

Begin by writing the question the procedure is intended to answer. Identify the information already supplied, the information it will produce and the information still missing.

These are constructed classroom examples; they do not require live accounts, private records or an external service. Do not treat a plausible explanation as a measured outcome.
