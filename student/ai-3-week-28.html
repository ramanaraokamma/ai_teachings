Main lesson · Section 2

Minimise first, then trace the data you retain

A deleted screen row is not evidence that every copy was removed.

Connect purpose, fields and stores

Privacy by design starts before collection: name the task purpose and decide whether each field is necessary. Less unnecessary content means fewer copies to protect and delete. For kept data, define who may access it, where it is copied, its retention boundary and what confirms deletion. A permission checkbox does not make unrelated collection useful.

These classroom policies are fictional design exercises, not legal retention rules or tests of a real service.

Retention and deletion cover all declared stores, including caches and logs. Removing a visible row may leave copies elsewhere.

Use fictional records only

Begin by writing the question the procedure is intended to answer. Identify the information already supplied, the information it will produce and the information still missing.

These are constructed classroom examples; they do not require live accounts, private records or an external service. Do not treat a plausible explanation as a measured outcome.
