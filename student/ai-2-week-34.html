Trace the total and tie slice

The script reports 5 and 6. About 83.3 percent of these checks pass, but that is not an accuracy estimate for all future use.

The cases were deliberately chosen, their frequencies are artificial, and each category has few cases.

The six development checks include ordinary, boundary, invalid, empty and tie cases. Five pass, but the only tie case fails. Saying five out of six pass is true; saying the tie rule works is false.

Keep both statements visible. A category table helps you choose the next repair. Adding many copies of passing ordinary cases can raise the overall fraction without checking a new condition or fixing the failed requirement.
