A frame for explaining

STRONG ANSWER FRAME I claim ______ because the evidence shows ______. The process is ______. One limit is ______.

A closer explanation

Check the claim against the facts

A deterministic model repeats the same prediction for the same input and settings. Ten identical runs do not give ten independent measurements of a smudged digit.

Several models can also share a feature gap, training examples or mistaken assumption. Agreement alone is not proof that the prediction is correct.

Keep the claim specific: tall under both listed heights, input still unclear, or no category until the measurement is checked. Do not attach a made-up confidence percentage.

Evidence about correctness and the meaning of confidence will be developed in Weeks 17 and 18.
