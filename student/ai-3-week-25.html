Understand criteria, anchors and gates

A rubric defines separate criteria and anchors: observable descriptions for each judgement. Correctness, usefulness, clarity, completeness and action boundaries ask different questions. Main lesson · Section 7

Find a point total used to bypass a gate

Repair the failed criterion and keep the original draft for comparison.

Repair the majority-of-checks shortcut

Claim to repair: Two of three checks passed, so release the answer. The declared rule requires all three, and factual support is mandatory. A count of passed criteria is descriptive; it does not override the release policy. A second shortcut is saying unsupported means false: the growth measurement is not established by S1, so omit or verify it rather than inventing proof that it never happens.
