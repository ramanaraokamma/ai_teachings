Understand coverage and case roles

An evaluation set is a recorded collection of inputs, permitted evidence and expected behavior. Include answerable, missing-source, ambiguous, conflicting and access-boundary cases that matter for the task. Development cases guide repairs; final reserved cases are kept from tuning. This lesson’s visible examples are practice, so they cannot serve as a hidden final test.

A small constructed set probes specific behavior; it is not a representative estimate of all future requests.

A system evaluation set contains cases with expected behaviour and scoring rules. It should cover retrieval, support, fallback and permissions rather than only easy answerable questions.

Extension · Ranking needs relevance judgements

A ranking needs task-specific relevance judgments and a declared cutoff. Precision and recall have different denominators.

Extension · Precision and recall definitions

Precision at k is relevant returned items divided by k when exactly k items are returned. Recall at k is relevant returned items divided by all known relevant eligible items.
