Apply the connected idea: Distribution shift

On development-like bright images, a frozen classroom classifier makes 2 errors in 20 cases:10 percent. On a separate dim set it makes 6 errors in 20:30 percent. The pooled result is 8/40=20 percent error.

The pooled score hides a 20-percentage-point difference between conditions.
