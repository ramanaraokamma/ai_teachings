A closer explanation

options = [{"name": "A", "fun": 4, "learning": 2, "minutes": 15},
           {"name": "B", "fun": 3, "learning": 5, "minutes": 25}]
for item in options:
    if item["minutes"] <= 20:
        score = 2 * item["fun"] + item["learning"]
        print(item["name"], score)

Only A is printed, with score 10. If B took 20 minutes it would qualify and have the higher score 11. The inclusive boundary matters.

Ratings and weights are invented for practice and require justification before use in a real decision.

Check must-have rules before scoring

Make two decisions in order. First check the duration: A takes 15 minutes and qualifies under the 20-minute limit; B takes 25 minutes and does not. Then calculate scores for eligible choices. A scores 2 times 4 plus 2, or 10.

B would score 11, but that score cannot remove the time requirement. Keep the rejection reason beside B instead of calling it a worse-scoring activity.
