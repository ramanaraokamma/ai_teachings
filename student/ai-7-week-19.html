Main lesson · Section 2

Distinguish access to an essay from permission to study it

Keep purpose, review, voluntary choice and handling visible.

Begin with the grading-versus-research question

Handing in work for grading answers a classroom requirement. It does not, under the supplied policy, grant permission to copy that work into a research study. Ask what people were told, what they could decline, what use was authorized and who reviewed the plan. Our activity uses fictional proposals only.

A well-written consent form is not by itself evidence that review, voluntariness, appropriate handling and the rest of the stated conditions have actually been satisfied.

Original ethics explanation

Research ethics considers purpose, burden, voluntary participation and consequences. Approval to teach does not automatically authorise a research data collection.

    Repair implied permission and unsupported approval claims

    State the missing process without pretending it has happened.

    Original qualification and repair setup

    This classroom policy exercise does not determine real legal or institutional requirements.

    A useful check asks what would make the reasoning fail. Distinguish a failure to execute the declared procedure from a weakness in the procedure itself.

    The first may require a correction; the second requires an explicitly revised method and new evidence. Preserve unsuccessful cases so an improved result cannot hide the original problem.
