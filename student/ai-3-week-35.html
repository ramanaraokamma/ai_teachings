Understand paired evaluation and revision

Evaluate a frozen candidate and baseline on the same declared cases and rubric. Keep task outcomes, source-access failures and resource measurements separate. Once you inspect a case to repair the system, it becomes exposed revision evidence; do not present it later as untouched final evidence.

Archive the original run, change one diagnosed component, rerun the exposed regression set and plan genuinely fresh reserved evaluation before a new final claim.

Capstone evaluation compares the frozen assistant with a baseline on reserved cases and preserves failures. Revision uses observed errors but needs fresh evidence for new final claims.

Extension · Cost, latency and quality are separate

Latency, cost and quality are distinct measurements. Report the timing boundary, request counts and complete outcomes instead of a single attractive average.
