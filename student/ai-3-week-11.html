Main lesson · Section 2

Define the relationship before judging the picture

What composition adds to object presence

Composition describes how objects are arranged and related: count, relative position, size, containment, visible labels and the source/destination of arrows. “Draw bottles and a beaker” names subjects but leaves key relationships unspecified. A clear brief turns the intended meaning into checks that a partner can apply. A more precise request is still not proof that an output follows it.

The complete main contract

Main fictional layout contract: exactly two labelled bottles B1,B2 left of one beaker; one arrow from the bottle pair toward the beaker. Use the supplied description, not an assumed experiment. Original description places the bottles right of the beaker and draws the arrow from beaker toward bottles. Count, left-of and semantic-flow checks are separate; no chemistry procedure is validated by this layout.
