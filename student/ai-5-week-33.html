Complete capstone question and prespecified candidate

A capstone needs a question that evidence can answer. “Build a better network” leaves better undefined.

Here the question is: does two-update full-batch fitting of one scalar ReLU weight reduce validation mean squared error relative to the initial-weight baseline on a constructed positive-input task?

Predict max(0, a*x), with no bias. The baseline has a = 1. The trained candidate starts at a = 1, uses learning rate 0.1 and exactly two updates. These choices are written before results are inspected.

Hypothesis, uncertainty and baseline scope

The hypothesis is that the trained candidate has lower validation MSE. The plan does not guarantee that result. This task tests training and record keeping, not whether neural networks are preferable to every other model.

A stronger comparison may also include an ordinary fitted line.

    Main lesson · Section 6

    Freeze the comparison and define when evaluation happens

    A written criterion says how a future result will be judged.

    Main validation/final criteria and versioned changes

    The validation criterion is candidate MSE strictly below baseline MSE on V1 and V2. Equality does not pass. The final criterion is lower MSE than the same baseline on the sealed final rows.

    Report both scores and every raw prediction whether or not the criterion passes. Record the rule, training rows, update count, learning rate, loss, data versions and code version.

    A criterion specifies how to judge a future result; it is not evidence that the result already happened.

    Keep validation and final criteria separate. Do not silently add epochs or change preprocessing after seeing a weak result. A changed plan is a new version and needs an honest account of what evidence informed it.

    Main lesson · Section 9

    Audit a complete plan without inventing an outcome

    Keep baseline calculations separate from unrun candidate and final results.

    Current baseline calculation task

    Compute baseline predictions, errors, squared errors and MSE on V1 and V2. State the denominator.

    Executable plan and complete baseline rows

    """Pre-experiment plan and baseline only; candidate and final results absent."""
import json
plan={'id':'CAP33-v1','question':'Does two-update fitting beat the initial-weight baseline?',
      'prediction':'max(0,a*x)','bias':False,'baseline_weight':1.0,
      'candidate_initial_weight':1.0,'learning_rate':.1,'updates':2,
      'batch':'full training set','loss':'MSE',
      'validation_rule':'candidate MSE strictly less than baseline MSE',
      'final_rule':'frozen candidate MSE strictly less than same baseline MSE',
      'candidate_result':'not run','final_result':'not measured'}
training=[('T1',1,2),('T2',2,4)]
validation=[('V1',3,6),('V2',4,8)]
ledger=[]
for name,x,y in validation:
    pred=max(0,plan['baseline_weight']*x)
    ledger.append((name,x,y,pred,pred-y,(pred-y)**2))
baseline_mse=sum(r[-1] for r in ledger)/len(ledger)
assert baseline_mse==12.5
print(json.dumps(plan,indent=2))
print('Training IDs:',[r[0] for r in training])
print('Validation: ID,x,target,prediction,error,squared error',ledger)
print('Baseline validation MSE:',baseline_mse,'denominator:',len(validation))
print('Candidate criterion: MSE <',baseline_mse,'on these same validation rows')
print('Candidate has not been fitted by this script; hypothesis remains untested.')
print('Final inputs/targets intentionally absent from this planning script.')
# Separate supported example, not the main baseline:
assert ((2-5)**2+(4-7)**2)/2==9
