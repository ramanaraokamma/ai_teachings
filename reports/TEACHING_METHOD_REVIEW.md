# Teaching-method review scope

This revision applies the learning cycle to every one of the 252 main weeks and 12 readiness lessons. Existing original and integrated content remains in the canonical role sections. The source-preservation validator checks all 144 original weeks and all 252 later source lessons.

Each selected assessment has a prompt in the student lesson and workbook, a matching teacher key, a support record, and an explanation/trace/limits rubric. The 264 selected prompts are distinct. Thirty-three repeated source lessons use changed parallel cases; all twelve Python readiness lessons use new cases with independently checked stdout. This does not establish a learner has never encountered a case outside this curriculum.

The individual misconception checks require case evidence rather than a yes/no vote. The teacher plan gives specific support actions for missing facts, wrong procedure, arithmetic/implementation mistakes, unsupported results and overgeneralised claims. Connected topics retain their own completion practice and individual check; the primary three-session allocation does not claim to cover all connected material.

Suggested retrieval and return intervals are scheduling choices. No learner outcomes have been invented. The blank classroom pilot record supports a real trial; age suitability, pace and impact require learner observation.

The current browser cannot launch in the restricted workspace. Previous browser reports are archived and do not verify revised HTML. Current PDFs use PyMuPDF; long tables are printed as labelled rows to avoid lost continuations. Text checks cover every current Word and PDF role edition. Word pagination has not been rendered. Representative PDFs receive visual inspection, not every page.
