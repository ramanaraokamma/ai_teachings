Extension · Chunk boundaries and exceptions

Chunking divides documents into retrievable units. A useful chunk retains enough structure to interpret a passage, including headers and exceptions.

Give each chunk its document/version ID, section and offsets. Preserve qualifiers or link adjacent material. Overlap can repeat information; repeated chunks are not independent sources.

Guided: The first chunk alone lacks the applicable exception. Retrieve the linked condition and determine maintenance status before asserting capacity 20.

Qualification: Smaller chunks can improve selectivity while losing necessary qualifiers; chunk size alone is not retrieval quality.

Exit: accept a concrete variation with its predicted outcome and a stated condition that would challenge the mechanism; reject a claim resting on unmeasured evidence.

Extension · Toy lexical and dot-product ranks

Lexical retrieval matches terms; vector retrieval compares numerical representations. Each can fail when the representation misses the relevant distinction.

For a toy lexical score, count distinct query terms present after lowercase exact-word matching. For a toy vector score use a stated dot product. Compare relevance using independent judgments, not score magnitude alone.

Guided: Lexical ranks D1 first; dot product ranks D2 first (.8 versus .4). The supplied task relevance favours the sensor manual; vector ranking alone does not prove relevance.

Qualification: Similarity and relevance are different quantities; exact terminology, metadata and permissions may be necessary.

Exit: accept a concrete variation with its predicted outcome and a stated condition that would challenge the mechanism; reject a claim resting on unmeasured evidence.

Main misconceptions

The worked result establishes every future case: Grounding requires support for the exact claim, including conditions; attaching a source name is insufficient.

A missing input may be replaced without recording the change: A replacement changes the evidence or contract. Record the change and its justification; do not silently turn unknown into a measured value.

Repeating an assessed case is new independent evidence: A replay checks consistency. An example used for feedback or revision cannot later be described as untouched final evaluation.
