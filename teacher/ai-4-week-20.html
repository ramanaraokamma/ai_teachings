Constant-fitting contract and source

Before a complex regression model, fit a simple constant predictor. A mean baseline uses the training average. Sort the training targets for a median: use the middle value for an odd count, or the average of the two middle values for an even count. The methods ignore query features but provide a reproducible reference. Evaluation targets must not determine the constants.
