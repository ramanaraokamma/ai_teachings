Explain the added worked example · 1

Before a complex regression model, fit a simple constant predictor. A mean baseline uses the training average. A median baseline uses the middle training target.

W1 Fit two constants

Workbook question · W1

Find the mean and median of the worked training targets.

Teacher reasoning · W1

Teacher explanation: Mean 6; median 5.

Teacher reasoning · W1

For the mean baseline, sum the worked training targets and divide by their count to obtain 6. For the median baseline, sort the training targets and take the middle value, 5.

Freeze these choices before examining evaluation targets; choosing a new constant from evaluation outcomes would contaminate the comparison.

W2 Score separately

Workbook question · W2

Calculate MAE for both baselines on [4, 8].

Teacher reasoning · W2

Teacher explanation: Both score 2; their per-record absolute errors differ.

Teacher reasoning · W2

For constant 6, absolute errors on targets 4 and 8 are 2 and 2, so MAE is (2+2)/2 = 2. For constant 5, the errors are 1 and 3, so MAE is (1+3)/2 = 2.

Equal averages can hide different per-record errors; retain the error table as well as the summary.
