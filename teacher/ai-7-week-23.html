Picture trace and changed-condition key

Power is the probability of detecting a specified effect under a specified model and decision rule.

Repeated simulation can estimate it; the estimate depends on effect size, variability, design and test assumptions, not sample size alone.

Only 100 trials per design produce noisy estimates. They are fictional stipulated results, not executed simulation or evidence about pupils.

Change: Change the assumed effect or variability but keep the sample sizes.

Prediction: The detection fractions need new simulation; the resource costs stay 40 and 80.

Reason: Power depends on the data-generating assumptions, while this stated unit-cost calculation does not.
