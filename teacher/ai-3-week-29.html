What the learner must demonstrate

A project uses an image found through search. Its author is known but its licence is missing. Is naming the author enough to establish reuse permission? Describe a provenance record and next action.

Concept to explain

Responsible creation distinguishes source material, transformation, attribution, and generated content.

TermMeaning
copyrightLegal rights concerning original creative expression; the permitted uses depend on the applicable rules and permissions.
provenanceA record of where information or material came from and how it was changed.
attributionIdentifying the source or creator of material; this alone does not establish permission to use it.
licenseTerms granting specified permissions to use material or software.

Diagnose and reteach

For guessing, return to given facts and request a trace. For vocabulary without application, use one example and one non-example. For arithmetic errors, check the setup before the calculation. Finish with a different uncoached case before recording mastery.

Address these additional misconceptions

The worked result establishes every future case: Real legal outcomes depend on jurisdiction and facts; supplied licence terms define this classroom exercise, not universal copyright law.

A missing input may be replaced without recording the change: A replacement changes the evidence or contract. Record the change and its justification; do not silently turn unknown into a measured value.

Repeating an assessed case is new independent evidence: A replay checks consistency. An example used for feedback or revision cannot later be described as untouched final evaluation.

Assess and explain the answers

Worked case answer

Record creator, source, licence or permission, attribution requirements and modifications. Attribution alone is not permission; classroom context is not a universal exemption.

Independent question

You find an attractive illustration online with no license information. What should you record and check before reusing it in a public project?

Expected independent answer

Record its source and creator if known, check permission or license terms for the intended reuse, and choose an appropriately licensed alternative if rights remain unclear.

Feedback

Ask the learner to find the evidence that challenges this claim: “Being visible online grants permission for any reuse.” Keep the first attempt and an uncoached retry separate.

Original transfer answer

No. Record creator, source URL, licence or permission evidence, required attribution and modifications. If permission is unclear, use an appropriately licensed alternative or seek guidance from the teacher. Do not assume search visibility or classroom use provides universal permission.
