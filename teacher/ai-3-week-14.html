State the claim-level verification procedure

Generated answers may invent facts, misread quantities or cite irrelevant passages. Verification checks each claim against evidence rather than accepting fluent wording.

Split an answer into atomic claims. For each, record the exact source passage, support status and any needed calculation. Mark unsupported and contradicted claims separately; do not repair them by inventing sources.

StatusQuestion to askWhat it does not imply
SupportedDoes the supplied passage or valid calculation establish this exact claim?Not a guarantee that the source is correct in the world
ContradictedDoes it conflict with the supplied evidence under the stated assumptions?Not proof of a model’s internal cause or intention
UnsupportedIs the required evidence missing or the inference unjustified?Not proof that the opposite claim is true

Diagnose the first misunderstanding

The worked result establishes every future case: Source agreement and arithmetic validity are separate checks; missing evidence is not proof of the opposite claim.
