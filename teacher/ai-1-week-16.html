How the mechanism works

UNDER THE HOOD Abstention is a decision policy: when confidence or evidence fails a threshold, the system defers.

Good thresholds balance coverage against error cost and specify what additional information or human review follows.

Explain the added worked example · 1

An unclear measurement, a missing feature and a weakly tested model create different problems. Saying the AI is unsure does not identify which one matters.

Write what is known, what remains unknown and how the unknown could change the result. Then choose a check that addresses that specific gap.

Use the familiar tower model: tall at a height of at least 6 cm, short otherwise, for whole-number heights from 1 through 10. A smudged card could read 5 cm or 6 cm. The threshold is known; the input is unclear.

For this paper investigation, treat the listed possibilities as the complete set allowed by the exercise. In real use, that assumption would itself need evidence.

At either 5 or 6 cm, one possibility predicts short and the other tall. Record no single category yet and ask for the original record or a new measurement. Do not select the more convenient digit.

Find the unsupported claim

CLAIM TO REPAIR Not sure always means the system has failed.

For guessing, return to given facts and request a trace. For vocabulary without application, use one example and one non-example. For arithmetic errors, check the setup before the calculation.

Finish with a different uncoached case before recording mastery.

Address these additional misconceptions

Two possible values imply equal probabilities: A list of possibilities supplies no probabilities unless further evidence states them.

Averaging guesses recovers the missing input: A calculated average is a new assumption, not an observed measurement.

Repeated identical outputs remove input uncertainty: Re-running unchanged information does not reveal a missing digit or unit.

A human reviewer can always settle the case: Require relevant evidence and allow an explicit unresolved outcome.

The rule and two stated possibilities are known; the actual height is unresolved. At 5 predict short, at 6 predict tall. Inspect the original readable record or remeasure.

W3 Choose useful review evidence

Workbook question · W3

A record says 60 but omits the unit. A reviewer suggests reading it as 60 cm because the model needs cm. Explain the problem, name the evidence to request and state the outcome if the unit cannot be established.

Teacher reasoning · W3

The required input format does not prove the original measurement’s unit. Ask for the original measurement record, instrument scale or recording instructions.

If the unit cannot be established, retain no prediction with reason unresolved unit; do not silently assume cm or convert from an assumed unit.

W4 Transfer the method to distance

Workbook question · W4

A paper model predicts far at a distance of at least 4 units, otherwise near, for whole-number distances 1 through 8. For each complete possibility set, compare predictions: 2 or 3; 3 or 4; 4 or 5.

Identify the set requiring review before a single category can be stated. Design a relevant check and an unresolved ending. Limit the claims for the other two sets.

Teacher reasoning · W4

At 2 or 3 both predict near. At 3 predict near and at 4 far, so that set leaves the category unresolved. At 4 or 5 both predict far.

Request a reliable distance reading in the stated units for the disputed set; if unavailable retain no single category and reason unresolved distance.

The other two predictions are stable only across their listed possibilities, not verified as correct labels or exact distances.

W5 Review the reviewer’s conclusion

Workbook question · W5

A reviewer cannot read the original tower card but chooses 6 cm because two models both predict tall when given 6. Explain why this fails to resolve the input. Write a more defensible review record and a next step.

Teacher reasoning · W5

Both models were given the same guessed input, so their agreement does not establish the actual measurement.

Record that the original remains unreadable, the height is unresolved and no supported single-category output is available. Request a reliable new measurement or keep the sorting decision on hold under the stated policy.

04. Change one thing. Trace what follows.

Original case: Ask a named reviewer to remeasure B using the specified width unit. Changed condition: Narrow B’s possibilities to 6 or 7 cm.

05. Connect the explanation to its limit

Uncertainty about a number need not always create uncertainty about the decision. Every allowed width for A fits, so its width uncertainty does not affect acceptance. B straddles the boundary.

Record both possibilities and request a better measurement instead of selecting the one that makes a preferred outcome. Limit: A review path is not automatically accurate; its conclusion depends on relevant, better evidence.
