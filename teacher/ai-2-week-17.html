Explanations for the additional workbook examples

W1 Compute a mean

Workbook question · W1

Find the mean of 4, 4, 6, 6 and 10.

Teacher reasoning · W1

Sum 30 divided by 5 gives 6.

Teacher reasoning · W1

Add all five observations: 4 + 4 + 6 + 6 + 10 = 30. Divide by the number of observations, five, to obtain a mean of 6. Keep the count beside the calculation.

Do not divide by the three distinct values; repeated observations still count.

Workbook question · W2

Teacher reasoning · W2
