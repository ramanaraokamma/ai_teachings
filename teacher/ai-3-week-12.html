Speech interaction includes capture, recognition, interpretation, and response stages.

TermMeaning
captureRecord a signal, observation or item of information for later use.
speech recognitionConverting spoken audio into an estimated sequence of words.

synthesisCombining information into a new account or output while preserving its meaning and support.

Diagnose the first misunderstanding

The worked result establishes every future case: A stage-level accuracy or authority policy does not establish every modality agrees or every downstream claim is supported.
